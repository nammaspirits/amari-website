# AMARI website v2 — Home page roadmap

Reference: nikka.com/en (calm, spacious, editorial), with AMARI's African identity.
Rules for every step: only the 6 brand colours · test on phone, tablet, desktop · preview locally first · publish only when approved.

Each step is small enough to build, check and publish on its own.

## Phase B — Page rhythm

- [ ] **Step 4 — Design system pass.**
  One spacing scale, one heading style (label → big title → short line → "Discover more" link), fewer coloured blocks: mostly Ivory / Desert Sand, Dark Cacao for one or two dramatic moments.
  *Check:* every section opens the same way; generous space between sections.

- [ ] **Step 5 — Trim and merge sections.**
  Target order: **Hero → The Bottle (3D) → Story → Taste & Serve → World of AMARI → Find AMARI → Footer.**
  Merge "The Seal" into Story, "Serve" into Taste, remove the duplicate bottle/cap images, keep the marquee band as one accent.
  *Check:* each bottle/cap image appears once; nothing important lost.

## Phase C — Section by section (one step each)

- [ ] **Step 6 — The Bottle (3D).** Restyle around the 3D bottle: bigger stage, cleaner controls, specs as a refined table.
- [ ] **Step 7 — Story.** Editorial layout: big quote-style heading, vision/mission, the seal as a single strong image.
- [ ] **Step 8 — Taste & Serve.** Three tasting notes + three serves in one section, possibly a horizontal scroll strip.

## Phase D — Motion and polish

- [ ] **Step 12 — Scroll reveals.** Headings rise in, images slide into place, one horizontal-scroll moment. Respect reduced motion.
- [ ] **Step 13 — Performance + accessibility check.** Image sizes, lazy loading, keyboard, contrast, screen reader labels.
- [ ] **Step 14 — Final cross-device review.** Phone, tablet, desktop, Brave/Chrome/Safari; fix anything found.
